Home Assistant

This app writes its own sensors into Home Assistant and plays books on its media players. Nothing is configured on the Home Assistant side: no YAML, no custom component, no restart. It needs the address of your Home Assistant and one long-lived access token made in it.

1 · The connection

The address you open Home Assistant at, with no dashboard path after it.

In Home Assistant: your own profile (bottom left) → Security → Long-lived access tokens → Create token. Copy it there and paste it here — it is shown once. It is kept in this app's database and never shown again here.

2 · What it publishes

Home Assistant forgets sensors written straight into it when it restarts, so a repeat keeps them there.

3 · Play a book on a media player

The players Home Assistant knows. Pick one, then carry on with a book: the playlist starts at the track you are on, and the app asks the player to skip to the second you stopped at.
